Frequently asked questions

How much will my job cost?

We inspect your site first, then send an itemised quote. The price depends on the size of the job, the materials and the wiring method. You approve it before we start.

Is the site inspection free?

Yes. Call or message us, tell us what you need, and we will arrange a visit to assess the work.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Woji, Port Harcourt and serve homes, businesses and industries around the city. Ask us about other locations in Rivers State.

Do you work in an emergency?

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency support for power faults. Call 0703 150 3838 any time.

Can you size a solar and inverter system for my needs?

Yes. We add up the appliances you need to run and for how long, then recommend the right panels, inverter and batteries for your budget.

Do you supply the materials or do I buy them?

We can supply quality materials and include them in your quote, or work with materials you have already bought.
